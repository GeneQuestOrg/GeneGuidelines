# ADR 007 — The diagnosis block is generated for every disease, checked by machines, and says nobody approved it

**Status:** accepted · 2026-11-24 (Darek's decision of 2026-11-23; implementation on `feat/guideline-points`, not yet deployed)

## Context

The parent map's block "What the guideline says about diagnosis – to show your doctor" existed only for FD: six points written by hand, each a verbatim guideline sentence, a plain summary in English and Polish, and one open question for the doctor. Darek decided (2026-11-23) that GeneGuidelines content is generated on demand for any rare disease, and that its quality comes from an automatic verifier loop and a plain "AI-generated, nobody approved it" notice, not from human review (ADR 008 in the product docs already says nobody signs a guideline off).

A lab (`scripts/labs/guideline_points/`) measured a generator→verifier loop on the whole catalog before anything was wired. Its first run found the loop correct but incomplete (FD lost its two most decision-changing points), the verifier inconsistent on questions, NCBI Bookshelf unreadable by machines, and one shelf holding another disease's chapters.

## Decision

- **The block is generated** by `backend/guidelines/points/` for every catalog disease except FD, whose page keeps the hand-written block. The hand-written block is the generator's worked example and the verifier's calibrated PASS example.
- **Two gates, both required.** A deterministic check: every quote is found word for word in the full text of the document it names (and is short when that document has no open licence). A separate verifier (Claude Opus, its own prompt) judges each quote in its surrounding paragraphs, the faithfulness of both summaries, the question and the Polish. A coverage review asks what a family needs at diagnosis that the draft leaves out. No point is published without both gates; a block is published only with at least three points.
- **Nothing is framed as approved.** The notice "generated by AI, nobody approved it" is fixed copy on every generated block; titles say "the guideline" only when every point comes from a consensus statement or practice guideline, otherwise "reviews of the literature". Every quote links to its source, and GeneReviews quotes carry the University of Washington credit its terms ask for.
- **Only sources about the disease, read in full.** Shelf documents about another disease are rejected (`backend/guidelines/relevance.py`); GeneReviews is read from NCBI's bulk archive (`bookshelf_chapters`), never generated from an abstract.
- **When it runs:** after each synthesis, when the shelf has changed (`guideline_points.shelf_hash`), and on an admin backfill.

## Consequences

- About 1 USD of Claude per disease per build; an unchanged shelf costs nothing.
- A generated block can still miss something a family needs; the coverage review reduces that, and the lab compares FD against the hand-written block to keep measuring it.
- GeneReviews' terms grant reuse "for noncommercial research purposes"; whether a foundation's patient-facing page fits that is a question for the foundation, recorded here rather than assumed.
